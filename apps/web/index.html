Does QoreWatch ever need my key or recovery phrase?

Never. You link by signing one transaction in QoreX, and watching needs nothing.

What does QoreWatch store about me?

Your chat id, the addresses you watch with their labels and settings, and which alerts it sent. /forget deletes all of it.

Can a team use one QoreWatch chat?

Add the bot to a Telegram group. Admins choose the addresses, and everyone sees the alerts.

What can QoreWatch not see?

Transactions QoreChain refuses at the door, like a classical-only signature, never reach a block, so no bot can report them.

Account check, scored out of 100

Send /check with any address or @handle. QoreWatch reads its post-quantum key, open windows, grants and token approvals, then lists a fix for each finding.

See a live check

Fixes are one tap from the alert. Revoking a permission, a fee allowance or a token approval opens a QoreWatch page where QoreX shows exactly what it signs.

Closing a window and removing session keys stay inside QoreX, which lets no website do them. For those, the page shows the three taps in the wallet and turns green when the chain confirms.
